## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-27

- **Q1 (Daily stand-up representation & overlapping ceremonies/meetings — Brief §7.1 & §7.3)**: *How should Daily stand-up and overlapping ceremonies/meetings be modeled?*  
  → **A1**:
  1. Represent Daily stand-up as a **single `SprintCeremony` record with `is_recurring_daily = true`**, applied to every working day of the associated Sprint and rendered as one `Daily` row in the Scrum Ceremonies table. Do **not** generate individual Daily records for every working day, and do **not** reintroduce reusable fixed-day `CeremonyTemplate`s.
  2. Other ceremonies and meetings remain date-specific and retain **date + duration only** (no time-of-day fields).
  3. For capacity calculations, sum applicable ceremony and meeting durations per Member per day, then clamp remaining available hours to `0`.
  4. Ceremonies apply to the whole Team; meetings apply only to their configured attendees (whole Team or selected Members).
  5. Treat event durations as additive planning deductions; actual clock-interval overlap detection is explicitly out of scope.

- **Q2 (Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction` & FTE ordering in Dev Hours and F004 engine — Brief §7.2 + F004 R5/R6/R8)**: *How should Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction` (F004) and FTE scaling reconcile in Team Calendar's Dev Hours view and F004 engine?*  
  → **A2**: Keep the distinction between Sprint-level overhead deductions and actual calendar event durations:
  1. Apply FTE **after** availability and Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction` deductions, preserving F004's agreed formula: `FTE × (Available − OverheadDeduction)`.
  2. Apply ceremonies and meetings as **actual clock-time deductions AFTER FTE scaling** — their durations are **not** multiplied by the Member's FTE factor (e.g., a 2 h ceremony deducts 2 h from a `0.5` FTE Member's 4 h day).
  3. Add planned overtime **AFTER FTE scaling** as actual additional hours (not multiplied by FTE).
  4. Keep Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction` separate in the Team Calendar presentation — do **not** distribute it artificially across daily cells or persist daily overhead deductions.
  5. Show the resulting reconciled Sprint `Dev h` total after accounting for FTE, Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction`, ceremonies, meetings, and planned overtime.
  6. Ceremonies apply to the whole Team; meetings apply only to configured attendees.
  7. Preserve existing zero-capacity clamping and advisory warning behavior. Both Capacity Planner (`/capacity`) and Team Calendar (`/calendar`) MUST consume the same underlying F004 capacity engine rules.

- **Q3 (Planned overtime boundaries & precision — Brief §7.4)**: *What boundaries and precision should apply to planned overtime (`extra_hours`)?*  
  → **A3**: Allow planned overtime on working weekdays, weekends, and public holidays:
  1. Allow planned overtime even when normal calendar availability is `0` (including weekends and public holidays).
  2. Use `0.25`-hour (`15`-minute) increments.
  3. Store durations as **integer minutes**, consistent with the canonical duration representation.
  4. Default the UI input to `2` hours (`120` minutes), without imposing `2` hours as a fixed allowance or limit.
  5. Apply overtime **after FTE scaling** as actual additional hours (never multiplied by FTE).
  6. Keep overtime separately identifiable from normal working capacity.
  7. If planned overtime overlaps with an active full-day absence on the same date, display an **advisory validation warning** rather than silently rejecting or modifying either record.

- **Q4 (Holiday Management placement & library holiday overrides on refresh — Brief §2.5, §3.5, §7.5)**: *Where should the Holiday Management view live and how should edits to public (`library`) holidays behave on refresh?*  
  → **A4**:
  1. Refresh only `library`-sourced records for the selected Location and year; preserve all `company` and `manual` records.
  2. Editing a library holiday creates a `manual` override rather than modifying the library-generated record directly, retaining a reference to the original library record or its stable source identity.
  3. Suppressed library records MUST NOT reappear during subsequent refreshes.
  4. Explicitly removing a library holiday MUST also suppress it from future refreshes.
  5. Refreshing library data MUST NOT overwrite or duplicate existing manual overrides.
  6. Location registry management remains owned by F002 (read-only in F005).
  7. **UI placement is provisional**: do not finalize the permanent Holiday Management route or Settings navigation as part of M0 (Settings architecture will be revisited post-M0 using Prototype v3 as design authority). Deliver the Holiday Management experience as a decoupled component/view accessible from the Team Calendar's Holidays card so it can be relocated cleanly later.

- **Q5 (Member × Day cell interactions & dynamic `AbsenceType` legend — Brief §2.2 & §2.4 vs. `LeaveCalendar.dc.html`)**: *How should Member × Day cell interactions and the Key/Legend align with F002's `AbsenceType` catalog?*  
  → **A5**:
  1. **Cell interactions**: Clicking any Member × Day cell opens a compact popover/modal supporting: adding/editing absences (date, type from F002 catalog, portion, planned/unplanned flag, optional note), adding/editing planned overtime, and an explicit **Cancel** action that sets absence `status = 'cancelled'`. Clicking an occupied cell MUST NOT immediately cancel or modify an existing record. When multiple markers occupy the same cell (e.g., holiday + overtime, AM + PM half-days, cancelled + active absence, or absence + overtime), the popover MUST let the operator inspect and act on each entry.
  2. **Key/Legend**: Dynamically derive absence-type entries from F002's `AbsenceType` catalog (`AL`, `SL`, `HD`, `BT`, `WFH`, `ON_SITE`), plus Planned Overtime, Cancelled, and relevant calendar markers (public/company holidays, weekends, today, ceremonies, milestones). Do **not** hardcode unseeded prototype codes (`Misc.` / `AWOL`). Future `AbsenceType` additions MUST appear in the legend automatically without F005 UI changes.

- **Pre-resolved Context (Brief §7.6 & §7.7)**:
  - **Dated Member-Team assignments (§7.6)**: Already owned by F002 (`member_team_assignments` with `start_date` and nullable `end_date`) and consumed by F004's engine; F005 reuses this canonical table without introducing a second assignment model.
  - **Commitment integration (§7.7)**: F005 precedes F012 (`PICommitment`) and F013 (`SprintCommitment`). Until those immutable records exist, `/calendar` displays `PI not started · capacity is live` and `Sprint not started` chips (with tooltip text clarifying `"No PI commitment snapshot exists yet (PI Commitments arrive in F012). Capacity reflects live calendar edits."` even when F002's `pi.lifecycle_status === 'in_progress'`), suppresses `vs PI` and `vs Sprint` columns, and creates **no** provisional commitment tables. The engine and calendar read model accept optional immutable commitment totals so F012/F013 can wire comparisons without rewriting F005.

### Session 2026-11-28 (Review Pass 1 Refinements)

- **R1 (Dedicated `planned_overtime` table & deprecation of `absences.extra_hours` — approved by operator 2026-11-29)**: Brief §2.9 references `Absence.extra_hours`, while `A3` requires canonical integer-minute storage, separate identifiability from normal working capacity, and coexistence with an active full-day absence (with an advisory warning). F005 therefore persists planned overtime in a dedicated `planned_overtime` table (`duration_minutes` integer in `15`-min steps) and **deprecates `absences.extra_hours`**: the column remains in SQL for upgrade compatibility, `CreateAbsenceSchema` rejects non-null `extraHours` (`400 VALIDATION_ERROR`), and any existing values remain untouched as unused legacy data with no migration or conversion.
- **R2 (Holiday override load-then-resolve order)**: Services load holidays matching either `(date BETWEEN start AND end)` OR `(overrides_library_key IS NOT NULL AND left(overrides_library_key, 10) BETWEEN start AND end)` plus `holiday_suppressions` for `original_date BETWEEN start AND end`. `engine.ts` runs `resolveEffectiveLocationHolidays(locationHolidays, locationSuppressedKeys)` **before** filtering to `assignedSet.has(date)` so a library holiday moved outside the Sprint or PI stops deducting capacity in its original window.
- **R3 (`/capacity` breakdown update & distinct event warning codes)**: Because `/capacity` consumes the same F004 engine with F005 ceremonies, meetings, and overtime, `/capacity`'s `BreakdownPopover` adds `Ceremonies & meetings` and `Planned overtime` rows and displays `max(0, max(0, Days − Leaves − deductions) × FTE − Events) + Overtime = Dev`. Separate warning codes are used: `'over_deducted'` (overhead > available), `'events_exceed_day'` (single-day events > daily post-FTE availability), and `'events_exceed_sprint'` (total effective events > post-FTE Sprint capacity). Existing `/capacity` numbers are unchanged at upgrade (zero ceremonies/meetings/overtime); subsequent ceremonies/meetings reduce `/capacity` dev days and Forecast SP by design.
- **R4 (Contiguous Sprint calendar window, CW bands & `devDaysLost` formula)**:
  - Each Sprint's rendered calendar window is `[scheduledStartDate .. calendarEndDate]`, where `calendarEndDate` extends through the trailing Sunday (`+2` days) when `scheduledEndDate` is Friday (clipped before the next Iteration's `scheduledStartDate`), giving 14/21/28 contiguous days for `offset = 0` and already-contiguous 14/21/28 days for `offset = -1` (Fri–Thu) and `offset = +1` (Tue–Mon) with zero gap weekends. ISO `CW##` headers span the exact count of rendered days in each ISO week (`grid-column: span <count>`).
  - `devDaysLost(h)` in the Holidays table is computed by `engine.ts` as the **FTE-weighted** sum across eligible `Team member`s in `h.locationId` assigned on weekday `h.date` of `m.fteFactor × (h.portion === 'full' ? 1.0 : 0.5)` (including unconfirmed `est.` holidays; `0` on weekends; holiday takes precedence over overlapping absences per F004 so neither is double-counted).
- **R5 (Member-level absence/overtime write guards & archived Team/PI read-only mode)**: Because `absences` and `planned_overtime` are Member-level (not Team/PI-scoped), API writes reject archived Members (`409 CONFLICT`), while archived Team/PI read-only mode on `/calendar` is enforced in the UI when `readOnly === true`. Exact absence write rules:
  - **Create** and **active edit/move/reactivate** (resulting `status === 'active'`): require active Member (`409`), Mon–Fri weekday (`400`), dated `member_team_assignments` covering `date` (`409`), and no overlapping active capacity-affecting absence (`409`).
  - **Cancel-only** (`{ status: 'cancelled', note? }` with no `date`, `absenceTypeId`, `portion`, or `planned` fields): requires active Member (`409`) and **skips** the dated-assignment and overlap checks so previously recorded absences can always be cancelled. On an **already-cancelled** absence, `{ status: 'cancelled' }` (no `note`) is a **`200 OK` idempotent no-op**, and `{ status: 'cancelled', note }` returns **`200 OK` updating `note` only**.
  - **Combined edit + cancel** (`status: 'cancelled'` alongside any `date`, `absenceTypeId`, `portion`, or `planned` field): **rejected (`400 VALIDATION_ERROR`)**.
  - **Other edits on an already-cancelled absence without reactivating** (`current.status === 'cancelled'` and `status` is neither `'active'` nor `'cancelled'`): **rejected (`409 CONFLICT`)** — cancelled absences are immutable unless reactivated (`status: 'active'`, which runs the full active-edit guards) or updated via `{ status: 'cancelled', note? }`.

### Session 2026-11-29 (Consolidated Review Pass 3 & Pass 4 Operator Approvals)

- **R6 (D-2 APPROVED — Darker milestone red `#D00000`, Deviation `D6`)**: Replace prototype `#FF0000` with **`#D00000`** with white text (~5.7:1 contrast, meeting WCAG AA 4.5:1) everywhere milestone red appears: date header (retaining precedence over `#F8CBAD` ceremony tint), Milestones table swatch, `MilestoneModal` badge, and Key/Legend entry.
- **R7 (Trailing-weekend read bounds, same-date `devDaysLost` deduplication & `weeksCount` definition)**:
  - Both `calendar-service.ts` and `capacity-service.ts` load `member_team_assignments` and `planned_overtime` over the full contiguous calendar window `[calendarStartDate .. calendarEndDate]` (up to `+2` days past Friday `scheduledEndDate`, including the PI's final Sprint) so trailing-weekend overtime and weekend-starting assignments produce identical totals on `/calendar` and `/capacity`.
  - When multiple effective holidays share `(locationId, date)` (e.g., `company` full + `manual` half), `engine.ts` computes the effective date loss once using F004's `max(portion)` rule (`1.0` if any `full`, else `0.5`), attributes it to the primary row (`full` > `half`, then `company` > `manual` > `library`, then `name` asc, then `id` asc), and sets `devDaysLost = 0` on secondary same-date rows so `Σ holidays[].devDaysLost === Σ over eligible Members m of (m.fteFactor × m.holidayDaysRaw)`.
  - Sprint length `weeksCount = Math.ceil(scheduledWeekdays / 5)` (separate from the number of rendered ISO `CW##` bands) so `compact = weeksCount >= 4` keeps normal-width columns for a `±1`-shifted 3-week Sprint spanning 4 `CW##` bands.
- **R8 (Holiday refresh retention, restore outcome & `updateTeam` / `updateIteration` ceremony-window guards)**:
  - `refreshLibraryHolidays` retains suppressed `library` rows in `holidays` (filtered on read by `resolveEffectiveLocationHolidays`) so `DELETE /api/calendar/holidays/suppressions/[id]` deletes the suppression row and checks the stored `library` row directly (no regeneration step). If the stored `library` row exists and is not shadowed, restore returns `{ restored: true, libraryRowAvailable: true, effective: true, effectiveReason: null, warning: null }`; if a `company` or `manual` row takes precedence on the same `(locationId, date)`, restore returns `{ restored: true, libraryRowAvailable: true, effective: false, effectiveReason: 'overridden_by_company_or_manual', warning: null }`; if the stored `library` row no longer exists (e.g., after a Location jurisdiction change), restore deletes the stale suppression and returns `{ restored: true, libraryRowAvailable: false, effective: false, effectiveReason: 'library_row_missing', warning: 'library_holiday_no_longer_generated' }` while preserving all overrides and other suppressions.
  - `updateIteration` (`cadence-service.ts`) guards **every** Iteration against date edits (`409 CONFLICT`) that would orphan dated `sprint_ceremonies` (direct edit to that Iteration), whereas `updateTeam` (`organization-service.ts`) guards only **current and future Iterations** (`scheduledEndDate >= today`) when `sprint_offset_workdays` changes so historical Iterations do not permanently lock a Team-wide offset setting.
- **R9 (Inter-Iteration gap overtime warning & no hardcoded absence-type codes)**: `PUT /api/calendar/overtime` returns `'outside_sprint_window'` as a non-blocking advisory warning when `date` falls in a real gap outside every active Team Iteration's contiguous calendar window. Zero `AbsenceType.code` values (including `HD`) are hardcoded anywhere; `portion` and `affects_capacity` govern deductions authoritatively.

## Problem Statement

F002 established organizational Entities (`Location`, `Holiday`, `AbsenceType`, `Absence`, `member_team_assignments`, and Team Sprint offsets) and F004 delivered the pure TypeScript Capacity Engine and `/capacity` planner, but Scrum Masters still have no operational screen to manage dated team availability, Sprint ceremonies, Team meetings, Team milestones, planned overtime, or annual Location holiday calendars. Without `/calendar` as the single source of truth feeding F004's capacity engine, leave and event deductions remain inaccessible to day-to-day planning, multi-location holidays cannot be refreshed or overridden safely, and M1/M2 planning views lack live daily availability and milestone markers.

## Scope

### In Scope

1. **Team Calendar UI (`/calendar`) — Days Off & Dev Hours Views**:
   - Replaces the F001 placeholder at `/calendar` (accessible from the **Capacity** navigation group).
   - Consumes the floating header's `ART › Team › PI › Sprint` context selector (extending header context with Iteration/Sprint selection on `/calendar`) and supports archived ART/Team/PI read-only inspection via URL query parameters (consistent with F004's deep-link behavior).
   - Displays the selected Team and Iteration using F002's Team-specific scheduled Sprint boundaries (`start_date`..`end_date` shifted by `teams.sprint_offset_workdays` ∈ `{-1, 0, +1}` weekdays) and supports **2-week, 3-week, and 4-week Sprints** (and arbitrary valid F002 weekday lengths) without hardcoding 10 working days.
   - **Header & toolbar**: `Team calendar` title, `Sprint planning` (`/sprint-planning`) and `Capacity planner` (`/capacity`) navigation links, `Days off | Dev hours` segmented view switch, concise usage instructions aligned with `A5`, and PI/Sprint commitment status chips (`PI committed` / `PI not started · capacity is live` and `Sprint committed` / `Sprint not started`, showing commitment dates in tooltips only when commitments exist).
   - **Member × Day calendar grid**:
     - Shows all Members assigned to the Team during the selected Sprint via dated `member_team_assignments` (full name / compact name on 4-week Sprints, initials/avatar, Location badge). Non-dev roles (`Admin`, `RTE`, `SM`, `PO`) appear in the calendar for attendance, leave, and meeting participation, clearly distinguished from dev-capacity-contributing `Team member`s.
     - Displays ISO calendar-week (`CW##`) grouping headers, month-boundary dividers, weekday/weekend columns, Today highlight (`#FFFF90`), Weekend (`#808080`), Holiday (`#C5E0B4`, with `est.` indicator for unconfirmed holidays), Ceremony/event date header (`#F8CBAD`), and Milestone date header (`#D00000` with white text, overriding ceremony tint).
     - **Days off view**: Renders visual availability markers for active/cancelled absences (planned vs. unplanned), attendance indicators (`BT`, `WFH`, `ON_SITE`), planned overtime, holidays, and weekends, with multi-marker visibility when entries coexist on the same date.
     - **Dev hours view**: Renders daily calculated development hours per eligible `Team member`, Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction` summary/column (`A2`), reconciled per-Member Sprint `Dev h` total, optional `vs PI` and `vs Sprint` columns (shown only when corresponding immutable commitments exist), and Team daily + Team Sprint `Overhead` + Team Sprint `Dev h` aggregated totals (summing eligible `Team member`s only). Non-dev roles (`Admin`, `RTE`, `SM`, `PO`) appear muted with a role pill, displaying **`—`** in daily and Sprint total cells (preserving holiday/absence/weekend tinting and tooltip `"Non-dev role (<role>) — excluded from development capacity"`), and contribute `0` to Team totals.
     - **4-week responsiveness**: Uses compact Member-name and total columns for ≥4-week Sprints, horizontal scrolling at narrower widths, and optional collapsed weekend dividers when tight.
     - **Dynamic Key / Legend**: Automatically derived from F002's `AbsenceType` catalog + Planned Overtime + Cancelled + calendar markers (`A5`).

2. **Dated Absence & Planned Overtime Management**:
   - Uses F002's `absences` and `absence_types` tables (`AL`, `SL`, `HD`, `BT`, `WFH`, `ON_SITE`); never hardcodes an absence-type enum.
   - Enforces `AbsenceType.affects_capacity` as authoritative (`AL`, `SL`, `HD` reduce capacity; `BT`, `WFH`, `ON_SITE` do not).
   - Clicking any Member × Day cell opens a compact popover/modal (`A5`) to:
     - Create or edit a dated absence (`date`, `absenceTypeId`, `portion`: `full | am | pm`, `planned`: boolean, optional `note` — no medical fields). Rejects undated entries; allows parking on a provisional working date and later moving the date without maintaining a separate move-history log.
     - Explicitly cancel an absence (`status = 'cancelled'`), preserving the record while removing its capacity deduction.
     - Create, edit, or remove **planned overtime** for a Member on any date (weekday, weekend, or public holiday) in `15`-minute (`0.25`-hour) increments stored as integer minutes, UI defaulting to `2` hours without enforcing a fixed 2-hour cap (`A3`).
     - Display an advisory validation warning (non-blocking) if planned overtime overlaps with an active full-day absence on the same date (`A3`).
     - Inspect and manage multiple entries occupying the same cell (`A5`).

3. **Sprint Ceremonies, Meetings & Team Milestones (2 × 2 Tables Below Grid)**:
   - Renders the four established tables in a 2 × 2 grid below the calendar: **Holidays** (top-left), **Milestones** (top-right), **Scrum ceremonies** (bottom-left), and **Meetings** (bottom-right).
   - **Sprint Ceremonies (`sprint_ceremonies`)**: Add, edit, and remove Team × Iteration ceremonies in chronological order (`Date`, `Ceremony`, `Duration` in `15`-minute integer-minute increments). Supports a single recurring Daily stand-up per Team × Iteration (`is_recurring_daily = true`, displayed as `Daily`) applied to every working weekday of that Sprint, plus date-specific ceremonies (`date` + `duration_minutes`, no time-of-day fields) (`A1`). Whole Team attends; dated ceremonies highlight date headers (`#F8CBAD`) and deduct clock-time duration in Dev Hours.
   - **Meetings (`meetings` + `meeting_attendees`)**: Add, edit, and remove Team meetings (`Date`, `Meeting`, `Who` — `Whole team` or attendee avatar/initials stack, `Duration` in `15`-minute integer-minute increments; no time-of-day fields) (`A1`). Deducts clock-time duration only from attending Members; meeting dates participate in calendar event header highlighting and tooltips.
   - **Team Milestones (`milestones`)**: Generic Team-level dated markers (`team_id`, `date`, `name`, `scope = 'team'`; no categories/types; zero capacity impact). Clicking a working date header opens the Milestone modal (empty date → Add Milestone; existing milestones → inline edit, remove `✕`, Add another, Done); table `+ Add` opens the same modal with a Sprint working-date selector. Multiple milestones allowed per date; solid red (`#D00000`, white text) header takes precedence over ceremony tint; header tooltip lists milestones and ceremonies.

4. **Canonical F004 Capacity Engine Extension & `/capacity` Breakdown Alignment**:
   - Extends `src/domain/capacity/` (pure TypeScript, zero DB/React dependencies) with unified top-level optional inputs (`ceremonies?`, `meetings?`, `plannedOvertime?`, `suppressedHolidayKeys?`, `commitments?` — replacing F004's unused `futureAvailability` placeholder) and `overridesLibraryKey?` on `Holiday`, so **both** `/calendar` and `/capacity` derive identical numbers from one authoritative formula (`A2`, `A3`, `R2`, `R3`):
     - **Holiday resolution order (`R2`)**: Run `resolveEffectiveLocationHolidays(locationHolidays, locationSuppressedKeys)` **before** filtering to `assignedSet.has(date)` so a library holiday moved outside the Sprint or PI is properly suppressed in its original window.
     - **Daily availability (per assigned weekday)**: `availableDayMinutes = workdayMinutes − holidayMinutes − effectiveAbsenceMinutes` (unchanged F004 overlap rules; `0` on weekends, full-day holidays, or full-day capacity-affecting absences).
     - **Sprint-level pre-FTE overhead**: `preFteMinutes = max(0, availableMinutes − overheadMinutes)` (emits `'over_deducted'` if `overheadMinutes > availableMinutes`).
     - **Post-FTE base**: `postFteMinutes = preFteMinutes × fteFactor`.
     - **Post-FTE clock-time ceremonies & meetings**: On each assigned weekday where `availableDayMinutes > 0`, sum applicable ceremony durations (including `is_recurring_daily` stand-up and dated ceremonies) plus attended meeting durations. Moving full-day leave/holiday onto an event day removes that Member's event deduction for that day. Cap each day's effective event deduction at `availableDayMinutes × fteFactor` (emitting `'events_exceed_day'` if events exceed daily post-FTE availability), then subtract from `postFteMinutes` and clamp at `0` (`netDevMinutesBeforeOvertime = max(0, postFteMinutes − effectiveCalendarEventMinutes)`, emitting `'events_exceed_sprint'` if clamped).
     - **Post-FTE planned overtime**: Add `overtimeMinutes` (sum of Member's planned overtime integer minutes within the Sprint's contiguous calendar window `[calendarStartDate .. calendarEndDate]` while assigned to the Team, including weekends and holidays, **not** multiplied by `fteFactor`): `devMinutesRaw = netDevMinutesBeforeOvertime + overtimeMinutes`, `devDaysRaw = devMinutesRaw / workdayMinutes`, `devHours = devMinutesRaw / 60`.
     - **Daily Dev Hours cells & Reconciliation (`A2`, `R3`)**: For each calendar day `d` of the Sprint (for eligible `Team member`s assigned on `d`), display `max(0, availableDayMinutes(d) × fteFactor − dayEventMinutes(d)) / 60 + overtimeMinutes(d) / 60` (on weekends/full holidays/full leave: `overtimeMinutes(d) / 60`, or `0` if none). Display Sprint-level `OverheadDeduction` separately at both Member and Team rows so the operator can audit `Daily sum − (OverheadDeduction × FTE) → Reconciled Sprint Dev h` (noting that when pre-FTE overhead or Sprint-level events clamp to `0`, the exact clamped equation `max(0, max(0, available − overhead) × FTE − effectiveEvents) + overtime` applies and is shown in the breakdown tooltip).
     - **`/capacity` BreakdownPopover update (`R3`)**: Updates `src/ui/capacity/BreakdownPopover.tsx` and `MemberCapacityTable.tsx` to display `Ceremonies & meetings` and `Planned overtime` rows, the full equation `max(0, max(0, Days − Leaves − deductions) × FTE − Events) + Overtime = Dev`, and distinct badges/messages for `'over_deducted'`, `'events_exceed_day'`, and `'events_exceed_sprint'`.
     - **Holiday `devDaysLost` (`R4`)**: Computed by the F004 engine as `Σ m.fteFactor × (portion === 'full' ? 1.0 : 0.5)` across eligible assigned `Team member`s in that Location on that weekday (including unconfirmed `est.` holidays; `0` on weekends; holiday takes precedence over overlapping absences per F004 so neither is double-counted).
     - **Optional Commitment Comparison**: Accepts optional immutable PI Commitment and Sprint Commitment totals keyed **per Iteration and per Member** in integer minutes (`byIteration: Map<iterationId, Map<memberId, minutes>>` + `committedAt`). For the selected Iteration `i`:
       - When `pi.byIteration.has(i)`: computes `vsPiHours(m, i) = liveSprintHours(m, i) − (piCommittedMinutes(i, m) / 60)` and **`teamVsPiHours(i) = teamSprintDevHours(i) − (Σ_{minutes ∈ pi.byIteration.get(i).values()} minutes / 60)`** — the committed side always sums the **immutable snapshot's roster** (including Members who later transferred out or were archived so lost capacity is never hidden), while live-only Members absent from the snapshot contribute to `teamSprintDevHours(i)` and are marked `New` rather than compared against `0`. (Identically for `vsSprintHours` / `teamVsSprintHours`.)
       - When `!pi.byIteration.has(i)` (e.g., an Iteration added after PI commitment): `vsPiHours = null`, `teamVsPiHours = null`, and `isNewSincePiCommitment = false` for all Members (UI shows `—` with tooltip `"Sprint not in PI commitment"`, never `New` and never compared against `0`; identically when `!sprint.byIteration.has(i)`).

5. **Multi-Location Holidays & Provisional Holiday Management Experience**:
   - **Team Calendar Holidays table**: Lists effective holidays within the selected Sprint for Locations represented by the Team's assigned Members (`Date`, `Holiday`, `Loc.`, `Dev days` lost from F004, with `est.` badge for unconfirmed holidays).
   - **Provisional Holiday Management view** (decoupled component/view accessible from the Holidays card header; UI route/placement explicitly provisional pending post-M0 Settings v3, `A4`):
     - Reuses `Settings.dc.html` (`07-settings-locations-holidays.png`) as visual reference: year selector, read-only F002 Locations overview (omitting `+ Location` and per-Location `Hours / day` inputs), full-year Month × Location holiday grid, `Refresh from public calendars` action, `+ Company holiday` action, and visual distinction for public, company, unconfirmed (`est.`), and weekend holidays.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001** *(AC01, AC02, AC16)*: `/calendar` MUST display one selected Team and Iteration using F002's Team-specific scheduled Sprint boundaries (`sprint_offset_workdays` ∈ `{-1, 0, +1}`), support 2-, 3-, and 4-week Sprints (and arbitrary valid F002 Iteration lengths) without hardcoded 10-day assumptions, and intersect Sprint dates with dated `member_team_assignments`.
- **FR-002** *(AC03, AC04, AC05, AC06, A5)*: System MUST manage dated absences using F002's `absences` and `absence_types` tables (`AL`, `SL`, `HD`, `BT`, `WFH`, `ON_SITE`), enforcing `AbsenceType.affects_capacity` authoritatively, rejecting undated entries (`400 VALIDATION_ERROR`), allowing date edits (moving placeholder dates without a move-history table), preserving cancellations with `status = 'cancelled'` (zero capacity impact), and opening a compact popover/modal on cell click without auto-cancelling occupied cells.
- **FR-003** *(AC03, A5)*: The Team Calendar Key/Legend MUST dynamically derive absence-type entries from F002's `AbsenceType` catalog (plus Planned Overtime, Cancelled, and calendar markers) without hardcoding prototype-only codes (`Misc.` / `AWOL`), automatically reflecting future `AbsenceType` additions.
- **FR-004** *(AC13, A3, R9)*: System MUST support planned overtime for a Member on any date (working weekdays, weekends, and public holidays) stored in `planned_overtime` as canonical **integer minutes** in `15`-minute (`0.25`-hour) increments (UI defaulting input to `2` hours without enforcing a fixed allowance/cap), applied **after FTE scaling**, kept separately identifiable from normal working capacity, and returning non-blocking advisory warnings when overlapping an active full-day absence (`'overlaps_full_day_absence'`) or falling in an inter-Iteration gap outside every active Team Iteration's contiguous calendar window (`'outside_sprint_window'`).
- **FR-005** *(AC10, A1)*: System MUST persist per-Team × Iteration `SprintCeremony` records supporting a single recurring Daily stand-up (`is_recurring_daily = true`, displayed as `Daily` and applied to every working day of the Sprint without generating per-day rows) and date-specific ceremonies (`date` + `duration_minutes` in `15`-minute integer-minute steps, no time-of-day fields), attended by the whole Team, displayed chronologically, and highlighting dated ceremony headers in `#F8CBAD`.
- **FR-006** *(AC11, A1)*: System MUST persist Team `Meeting` records (`date` + `duration_minutes` in `15`-minute integer-minute steps, no time-of-day fields) with whole-Team or specific-Member attendees (`meeting_attendees`), displaying `Whole team` or attendee avatars/initials, highlighting meeting dates, and deducting duration only from attending Members present on that date.

## Edge Cases

- **Full-day leave or holiday on a ceremony/meeting day**: Moving or adding a full-day capacity-affecting absence or full-day Location holiday onto a date with ceremonies or meetings sets `availableDayMinutes = 0` for that Member on that date and removes that date's ceremony/meeting deductions from their Sprint total (never deducting phantom event hours from other working days).
- **Half-day absence or half-day holiday on a ceremony/meeting day**: `availableDayMinutes = workdayMinutes / 2`, post-FTE base is `(workdayMinutes / 2) × fteFactor`, and clock-time ceremonies/meetings deduct from that post-FTE base, clamping at `0` with an advisory warning if events exceed it.
- **Ceremonies + meetings exceeding a day's post-FTE capacity**: Additive durations (`A1`) are capped per day at `availableDayMinutes × fteFactor` so one over-booked day clamps to `0` with an advisory warning rather than subtracting hours from adjacent days.
- **Planned overtime on weekends, holidays, inter-Iteration gaps, or overlapping full-day absence (`A3`, `R7`, `R9`)**: Allowed on Sat/Sun and public holidays (yields `+overtimeHours` on an otherwise `0`-hour day); trailing-weekend overtime inside `[calendarStartDate .. calendarEndDate]` is loaded identically by both `/calendar` and `/capacity`. If recorded on the same date as an active full-day absence, both records persist and `'overlaps_full_day_absence'` is returned; if recorded in a break week outside every active Team Iteration window, it persists and `'outside_sprint_window'` is returned.
- **Same-date effective holidays (`R7`)**: When multiple effective holidays share `(locationId, date)` (e.g., `company` full + `manual` half), `devDaysLost` is computed once using `max(portion)` (`1.0` if any `full`, else `0.5`), attributed to the primary row (`full` > `half`, then `company` > `manual` > `library`, then `name` asc, then `id` asc), and `0` on secondary rows so `Σ devDaysLost === Σ over eligible Members m of (m.fteFactor × m.holidayDaysRaw)`.
- **Cancelling an absence after assignment changes (`R5`)**: Cancel-only (`status: 'cancelled'` with optional `note` and no `date`/`absenceTypeId`/`portion` change) skips the dated-assignment and overlap checks so operators can always cancel historical/reassigned absences; combining `status: 'cancelled'` with `date`/`absenceTypeId`/`portion` changes is rejected (`400 VALIDATION_ERROR`); moving or editing an active absence requires a valid weekday and covering `member_team_assignments`.
- **Multiple markers in one Member × Day cell (`A5`)**: Examples include Location holiday + planned overtime, `am` + `pm` half-day absences, a cancelled absence + a newly created active absence, or `WFH` + planned overtime. The cell visually indicates the primary/combined state and its popover lists every entry on that date for inspection, editing, or cancellation.
- **Overlapping absences & holidays**: Preserves F004's non-double-deduction rule (`effectiveAbsence = min(rawAbsence, 1.0 − holidayFraction)`), with duplicate `am` or duplicate `pm` counted once (`0.5` day).
- **Mid-Sprint dated Team membership & transfers**: Only dates within the Member's `member_team_assignments` for the selected Team contribute to working days, ceremonies, meetings, and planned overtime on that Team.
- **Archived Members & past vs. current/future Sprints**: Consistent with F004, an archived Member assigned to the Team contributes to capacity only in Sprints with `scheduledEndDate < today`; in current/future Sprints they are excluded from dev totals and mutations targeting them are rejected (`409 CONFLICT`).
- **Non-dev roles (`Admin`, `RTE`, `SM`, `PO`)**: Appear in the Team Calendar for attendance, leave, and meeting attendee selection, clearly badged as non-dev, and contribute `0` to Team daily and Sprint `Dev h` totals.
- **Multiple milestones on one date**: All milestones for that date appear in the Milestone modal (inline edit + `✕` remove), in the Milestones table, and in the red date-header tooltip alongside any ceremonies on that date.
- **Library holiday edits, suppressions & refresh idempotency (`A4`)**: Editing a library holiday creates a `manual` override linked to the library identity; removing a library holiday records a suppression for that identity. Repeated refreshes for the same Location/year remain idempotent — never resurrecting suppressed library holidays, never duplicating manual overrides, and never touching `company` holidays. Respects F002's `library_seed_cutoff_date`.
- **Empty states**: Sprints with zero holidays, zero milestones, zero ceremonies, or zero meetings render clean empty states (e.g., *"None this sprint. Click a date in the calendar to add one."* for Milestones) without layout collapse.
- **Invalid or archived deep links**: Mirrors F004 — valid archived `?teamId=&piId=&iterationId=` opens read-only with `"Return to active selection"`; invalid/mismatched params are cleared with a dismissible notice while keeping the current active selection.

### 1. Persistence Conventions & Constitution Principle III

- All schema changes live in local PGlite managed by Drizzle ORM (`src/persistence/migrations/0004_team_calendar_availability.sql` + updated journal & snapshot), using F001's `auditColumns()` (UUIDv4 `text` IDs, UTC timestamps, `created_by`/`updated_by` defaulting to `'local:sm'`, never accepted from HTTP bodies) and `ON DELETE RESTRICT` on organizational/member foreign keys (`ON DELETE CASCADE` only on `meeting_attendees.meeting_id`).
