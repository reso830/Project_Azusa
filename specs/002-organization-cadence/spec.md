## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-23

- **Q1 (Organization UI entry point, Location editing, Header context & Today widget)**: *Where should F002's organization & cadence UI live, should Locations be editable, and should F002 activate the header context selector and Today widget PI/Sprint display?*  
  → **A1**: Use a hybrid of dedicated organization routing and live shell context:
  - **Organization UI**: Create a dedicated `/organization` route accessible through a new **`Organization`** entry in the profile dropdown. Keep **`Settings`** in the profile dropdown as a disabled, non-navigating placeholder (no standalone Settings application). Support ART, PI, Iteration/Sprint, Team, Member, and Location management on `/organization`, including **creating and editing Locations** in addition to the initial seeded Locations.
  - **Header context**: Activate the **`ART › Team › PI`** context selector in the floating header in F002, and update the profile dropdown subtitle to reflect the selected organizational context (e.g., `Team · ART`, falling back to `No ART or team configured` when none is selected/configured).
  - **Today widget**: Retain the existing viewer-local date, time, and ISO calendar week, and display the **selected PI and current Sprint** resolved from configured dates and the selected Team's working-day offset. Do **not** imply that the PI or Sprint has been formally committed (PI Commitment and Sprint Commitment remain separate lifecycle actions in F012 and F013).
  - Keep F002 strictly limited to organization and calendar context; do not introduce capacity calculations or planning-board functionality. *(Refined by OD-2, OD-5, OD-6, and OD-7 below.)*

- **Q2 (Working-day calendar for Team ±1 working-day Sprint offsets)**: *How should the ±1 working-day Team Sprint schedule offset be calculated across multi-location teams?*  
  → **A2**: **Weekdays only (Monday–Friday), excluding Saturday and Sunday.** The Team Sprint offset is an operational scheduling mechanism for staggering ceremonies and must not depend on any primary Location's holiday calendar. Member-specific public holidays and absences are applied individually during capacity calculations in F004 via `NETWORKDAYS`; a local holiday reduces an individual member's availability rather than shifting the Team's Sprint schedule.

- **Q3 (PI and Iteration date-overlap & containment rules)**: *What date-overlap and containment constraints should apply to PIs and Iterations within the same ART?*  
  → **A3**: Enforce strict non-overlap and containment on the canonical ART calendar:
  - Active PIs within the **same ART** must not overlap. *(Note: overlap checks consider `record_status = 'active'` PIs only, so an archived PI may overlap an active PI; F004 and downstream planning views must filter by `record_status = 'active'` unless inspecting archived history.)*
  - Iterations within the **same PI** must not overlap.
  - Every Iteration must fall **entirely within** its parent PI's start and end dates (`pi.start_date <= iteration.start_date <= iteration.end_date <= pi.end_date`).
  - **Different ARTs** maintain independent calendars, so their PIs and Iterations may overlap freely.
  - **Gaps** between PIs or between Iterations are permitted; contiguous scheduling is not mandatory.
  - Apply these constraints to the **canonical ART calendar**; Team-specific `−1 / 0 / +1` working-day offsets are separate operational schedules and must never mutate canonical PI or Iteration identities or dates.

- **Q4 (Initial AbsenceType catalog & Member role values)**: *Which initial AbsenceType entries and concrete Member role values should F002 seed?*  
  → **A4**:
  - Seed **six `AbsenceType` records**: `AL` (Annual leave, `affects_capacity: true`), `SL` (Sick leave, `affects_capacity: true`), `HD` (Half day, `affects_capacity: true`, follows its configured portion), `BT` (Business trip, `affects_capacity: false`), `WFH` (Work from home, `affects_capacity: false`), and `ON_SITE` (On-site, `affects_capacity: false`). `WFH` and `ON_SITE` are attendance indicators and do not reduce capacity.
  - Enforce **five concrete Member roles** via domain enum and SQL `CHECK` constraint: `Admin`, `RTE`, `SM`, `PO`, and `Team member` (`PO` and `SM` are excluded from development capacity; `Admin`, `RTE`, and `Team member` count toward development capacity). Exclude `Business Owner` for now; it remains tentative in the conceptual model and can be introduced later if required.

- **Q5 (Lifecycle, deactivation & deletion rules)**: *How should deactivation and deletion work for ARTs, Teams, Members, and PIs without losing historical integrity?*  
  → **A5**: Use **soft-deactivation/archive** (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`) for ARTs, Teams, Members, PIs, and Locations *(extended to Locations by OD-3, and kept distinct from `PI.lifecycle_status`)*:
  - Archived records remain available for historical inspection and reporting.
  - Archived records cannot receive new planning assignments.
  - **Hard deletion** is permitted **only** for unreferenced records created by mistake; never cascade-delete historical records, commitments, plan versions, or actuals.
  - Archiving a parent record (e.g., an ART or Team) must **not** automatically archive its child records.
  - Allow **reactivation** (`archived` → `active`) where appropriate, subject to domain validation (parent must be active; reactivating an archived PI must satisfy non-overlap constraints within its ART).

### Session 2026-11-23 (review)

- **OD-1 (Sprint dates are weekdays)**:
  → Iteration `start_date` and `end_date` MUST both fall Monday–Friday. Saturday or Sunday is rejected with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` in both the domain validator and API. Canonical ART dates remain the official schedule. Because Sprints within a PI cannot overlap or touch and every boundary is a weekday, a ±1 weekday shift strictly preserves order and guarantees no two shifted Sprints of a Team can ever share a day (including across consecutive PIs in the same ART).
- **OD-2 (Today widget uses Team-shifted dates)**:
  → The Today widget matches today's viewer-local date against the selected Team's shifted Sprint dates, including days just outside the PI's canonical range (e.g., a `-1` Team shows Sprint 1 on the Friday before the PI's canonical Monday start, and a `+1` Team shows the last Sprint on the Monday after the PI's canonical Friday end). As a safety-net tie-break (only if crafted/invalid data yields two shifted Sprints containing today), select the Sprint whose canonical range contains today (and earliest canonical start date if still tied).
- **OD-3 (Locations can be archived)**:
  → Locations use the same `record_status` (`active` | `archived`) as ARTs, Teams, Members, and PIs. An archived Location is hidden from the Member Location picker, and assigning it to a new Member or moving a Member to it is rejected with `409 CONFLICT`. Existing Members keep their archived Location and its holidays remain preserved. Archived Locations remain visible on `/organization` → Locations behind `"Show archived"` with their holidays viewable, and can be reactivated. Hard delete stays blocked while any Member or Holiday references the Location.
- **OD-4 (Members can move Teams with minimal dated history)** *(superseded in part by OD-8, OD-9, and OD-11 below: `members.team_id` is removed, `member_team_assignments` is the sole date-resolved source of truth, future moves / same-day corrections / cancel pending move are supported, and backdating `< today` is prohibited)*:
  → `teamId` is editable on Member `PATCH` with an optional `effectiveDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, defaulting to today). The destination Team must be active (`record_status = 'active'`), otherwise `409 CONFLICT`. Moving across ARTs is allowed, and archived Members may be moved to an active Team. Role, FTE, Location, and Absences stay with the Member. Deleting an unreferenced Member removes its own assignment rows; a Team cannot be hard-deleted while any assignment row (past, current, or pending) references it.
- **OD-5 (Sprint label in the Today widget)**:
  → Render `<PI> · Sprint <name>`, skipping the `Sprint ` prefix when `<name>` already starts with `"Sprint"` (case-insensitive; e.g., `2706` → `Sprint 2706`, `Sprint 5` → `Sprint 5`). No separate IP marker. This formatting rule lives in the domain (`resolveTodayCadenceStatus`), not in UI components.
- **OD-6 (Header on `/organization` matches the Settings prototype)**:
  → On `/organization`, the floating header displays only the logo, expandable search (with page-specific placeholder `"Search organization…"`), and profile menu — **no `TodayWidget` and no `ART › Team › PI` context selector**, matching `prototype/Settings.dc.html`. The profile dropdown subtitle still reflects the current organizational context.
- **OD-7 (Header ART stays plain text, faithful to `Main.dc.html`)**:
  → In the floating header on primary routes, the ART label is plain text linking to `/program-board` (`prototype/Main.dc.html#L36`). The Team dropdown lists active Teams grouped under ART headings; selecting a Team sets its ART, and the PI dropdown then shows that ART's active PIs. An ART with zero active Teams does not appear in the header (its PIs are still managed on `/organization`).
- **OD-8 (Future-dated Team moves & date-resolved Team membership)**:
  → `member_team_assignments` is the sole source of truth for Team membership (`members.team_id` is removed). A Member's Team on date `D` (defaulting to server local today) is the row with `start_date <= D` and (`end_date IS NULL` or `end_date >= D`). Roster queries are date-based. At most one pending row (`start_date > today`) is permitted per Member; a new move's `effectiveDate` must be `>=` the latest row's `start_date` (`>` opens a new row, closing the prior row at `effectiveDate - 1 day`; a second pending row is rejected with `409`). Provide a **cancel pending move** operation that deletes the future row and reopens the previous row (`end_date = NULL`). Archiving a Team that has pending incoming moves (`start_date > today`) is rejected with `409`. F004 MUST use date-aware membership lookups from `member_team_assignments` (no longer deferred).
- **OD-9 (Same-day correction for Team assignments)**:
  → If `effectiveDate` equals the latest row's `start_date` (whether current or pending), update that row's `team_id` in place. When correcting today's move back to the preceding active Team, delete today's row and reopen the preceding assignment. The destination Team must be active (`409` otherwise). If `effectiveDate < latestRow.start_date`, reject with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
- **OD-10 (Absence conflicts apply only to capacity-reducing types)**:
  → Absence conflict rules (`full` blocks any other capacity-reducing absence on the same `(member_id, date)`; duplicate active `am` or `pm` blocked) apply **only** among active Absences whose `AbsenceType` has `affects_capacity = true` (`AL`, `SL`, `HD`). Attendance-only types (`WFH`, `ON_SITE`, `BT`) never conflict with any other Absence.
- **OD-11 (No backdating on Team moves — aligns with Non-Goal 9)**:
  → Reject any Member move with `effectiveDate < today` with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` so past rosters cannot be rewritten retroactively. Same-day corrections (`OD-9`, `effectiveDate === latestRow.start_date`) are therefore permitted **only** for a row starting `today` (same-day mistake) or a pending future row (`start_date > today`). Correcting a pending move back to the immediately preceding (original) Team is treated as **cancel pending move** (delete the pending row and reopen the preceding row with `end_date = NULL`) so two consecutive rows for the same Team are never created. Submitting a move dated before an existing pending move (`today <= effectiveDate < pending.start_date`) is rejected with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` stating that the pending move must be cancelled first.

## Problem Statement

Before Azusa can support work categories (`F003`), capacity forecasting (`F004`), dated team calendars (`F005`), or Team/ART PI planning (`F006`–`F019`), it needs authoritative organizational and calendar foundations (`F002`). Without explicit, domain-enforced models and management flows for ARTs, Teams (with permitted ±1 working-day Sprint offsets), unlimited Member rosters with roles/FTE/Locations and Team-transfer history, reusable Locations with IANA time zones and seeded `date-holidays` public holidays, dated Absence/AbsenceType reference records, and configurable weekday-bounded PI/Iteration cadences—alongside live header context selection and Today-widget date resolution—downstream features would either hardcode sample cadences, duplicate organizational tables, or break historical integrity when records change.

## Scope

### In Scope

1. **ART (Agile Release Train) Management**:
   - Create, edit, list, archive/reactivate (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`), and hard-delete (only when unreferenced) ART records.
   - Each ART has a case-insensitively unique trimmed name, owns its independent PI and Iteration cadence, and serves as the organizational parent for its Teams and PIs.
2. **Team Management & Permitted Working-Day Offset**:
   - Create, edit, list, archive/reactivate (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`), and hard-delete (only when unreferenced by any Member or `member_team_assignments` row, past or current) Teams belonging to **exactly one ART** (`trainId` immutable after creation).
   - Configure `sprint_offset_workdays` strictly within `{-1, 0, +1}` working days (weekdays Mon–Fri only, skipping Sat/Sun).
   - Preserve the Team's default `hours_per_day = 8.0` in the domain and persistence model (without implementing capacity calculations or workday-hours editing UI in F002).
   - Derive Team-specific scheduled Sprint start/end dates from canonical ART Iteration dates + `sprint_offset_workdays`, keeping Team scheduled boundaries clearly distinct from canonical ART Iteration identity and dates.
3. **Member Roster Management, Date-Resolved Team Moves & Role Placeholders**:
   - Create, edit, move between active Teams (current, future-dated, or same-day correction; even across ARTs), cancel pending future moves, list (resolved by date `D`, defaulting to server local today), archive/reactivate (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`), and hard-delete (only when unreferenced by Absences) Members with no fixed roster-size cap.
   - `member_team_assignments` is the sole source of truth for Team membership (`members.team_id` is removed). On date `D`, a Member belongs to the assignment row with `start_date <= D` and (`end_date IS NULL` or `end_date >= D`). At most one pending future row (`start_date > today`) per Member is permitted.
   - Store conceptual Data Model v0.6 attributes: `full_name`, `nickname`, `initials`, `role` (`Admin` | `RTE` | `SM` | `PO` | `Team member`), `fte_factor` (default `1.0`, `> 0` and `<= 1.0`), `location_id`, and `avatar_color`.
   - Preserve the domain rule that `PO` and `SM` roles do not contribute to development capacity (`contributesToDevCapacity(role)`), while `Admin`, `RTE`, and `Team member` count toward development capacity, without computing capacity in F002 and without authentication or RBAC enforcement.
   - Retain F001's UUIDv4 PKs and `created_at`/`updated_at`/`created_by`/`updated_by` (`"local:sm"` default, internal-only actor override) across all new tables.
4. **PI & Configurable Iteration / Sprint Cadence Management**:
   - Create, edit, list, archive/reactivate (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`), and hard-delete (only when unreferenced) PIs belonging to **exactly one ART**, with explicit `name` (case-insensitively unique within ART), explicit `start_date`, explicit `end_date`, and a separate `lifecycle_status` (`planning` | `in_progress` | `closed`, defaulting to `planning`; transitioned by F012).
   - Enforce non-overlapping dates among active PIs within the same ART while permitting gaps and permitting overlapping PIs across different ARTs.
   - Configure Iterations (Sprints) belonging to **exactly one PI**: configurable count of `development` Sprints, optional `ip` iteration, configurable duration (not restricted to 2 weeks), explicit `name` (case-insensitively unique within PI), `kind` (`development` | `ip`), and explicit **Monday–Friday weekday** `start_date` / `end_date` (weekends rejected with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`).
   - Enforce non-overlapping Iteration dates within the same PI and full containment within the parent PI's date range, while permitting gaps.
   - Provide an optional `YYWW` (ISO-8601 ending-week) naming helper/suggestion while allowing arbitrary user-supplied names; dates are always authoritative and never derived from names.
5. **Location Registry, Holiday Reference Seed & Absence Reference Model**:
   - Reusable, shared `Location` registry (`code` case-insensitively unique and immutable after creation per `R2-2`, `name`, ISO 3166-1 `country_code`, optional ISO 3166-2 `subdivision_code`, canonical IANA `time_zone`, `calendar_source`, `last_refreshed_at`, `seeded_years`, `library_seed_cutoff_date`, `record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`), **not** owned by individual Teams.
   - Seed the 6 initial Locations from the handoff (`PH`, `SG`, `AU-WA`, `IN`, `UK`, `US-TX`) with canonical IANA time zones (`Asia/Manila`, `Asia/Singapore`, `Australia/Perth`, `Asia/Kolkata`, `Europe/London`, `America/Chicago`).
   - Support creating, editing (excluding immutable `code`), archiving/reactivating, and unreferenced-only deleting Locations on `/organization`.
   - `Holiday` persistence and domain model associated with `Location` (not Team): `date`, `name`, `portion` (`full` | `half`), `source` (`library` | `company` | `manual`), and `confirmed` (boolean, with a concrete Hijri/Islamic moon-sighting rule setting `confirmed = false`).
   - Seed public/bank holidays for the initial supported Locations using `date-holidays` (ISC), preserving provenance so `company`/`manual` overrides supersede `library` rows on the same date without deleting them, tracking `seeded_years` and `library_seed_cutoff_date` so startup/rollover seeding never inserts library rows before a jurisdiction-change cutoff (`R2-1`).
   - `AbsenceType` persistence and domain model (`code` case-insensitively unique, `name`, `color`, `affects_capacity`), seeded with the 6 initial types (`AL`, `SL`, `HD`, `BT`, `WFH`, `ON_SITE`).
   - `Absence` persistence and domain model associated with `Member` and `AbsenceType`: required `date` (no undated/floating absences), `portion` (`full` | `am` | `pm`), `planned` (boolean), `status` (`active` | `cancelled`), optional `extra_hours`, optional `note`, rejecting conflicting active **capacity-reducing** (`affects_capacity = true`) Absences for the same Member and date while allowing attendance-only types (`WFH`, `ON_SITE`, `BT`) without conflict (`OD-10`).
6. **Dedicated `/organization` Surface & Live Shell Context**:
   - Dedicated `/organization` route using the existing Azusa shell and Kumiko design system, providing focused management sections for **ARTs & PI Cadence**, **Teams & Members**, and **Locations** (including read-only inspection of seeded holiday records per Location and `"Show archived"` toggles).
   - On `/organization`, the floating header renders **only** the logo, search (placeholder `"Search organization…"`), and profile menu — omitting `TodayWidget` and the `ART › Team › PI` context selector (`OD-6`).
   - Add an active **`Organization`** link to the profile dropdown navigating to `/organization`; keep **`Settings`** disabled as a non-navigating placeholder; highlight no primary navigation menu item while on `/organization`.
   - On the 9 primary routes, activate the **`ART › Team › PI`** context breadcrumb in the floating header (plain-text ART link to `/program-board`, Team dropdown grouped by ART headings, PI dropdown for that ART; omitted when no active Teams exist), update the profile dropdown subtitle to show the selected `Team · ART` (or `"No ART or team configured"`), and update the Today widget to display `<PI> · Sprint <name>` (skipping `Sprint ` if `<name>` already begins with `"Sprint"`, case-insensitive) matched against the selected Team's shifted Sprint dates without implying formal commitment.
7. **Automated Verification & Living Documentation**:
   - Deterministic Vitest unit and integration tests covering all domain invariants, weekday/offset math, golden 2027 holiday fixtures, seed idempotency, override/jurisdiction rules, migration/backup/restore compatibility, and API validation.
   - Playwright E2E and `@axe-core/playwright` accessibility tests covering `/organization`, reload persistence across edits, the profile dropdown, header context switcher, and live Today widget.
   - Update `README.md`, `docs/architecture/overview.md`, `docs/architecture/domain-model.md`, `docs/architecture/data-dictionary.md`, `docs/operations/database-and-recovery.md`, `docs/REPO_MAP.md`, ADR `0002`, and `CHANGELOG.md`.

### Non-Goals (Out of Scope)

1. **No Standalone Settings Application**: Do not enable the profile-menu `Settings` item or create a `/settings` route or multi-section Settings app.
2. **No Authentication, Multi-User Accounts, or RBAC Enforcement**: Role values (`Admin`, `RTE`, `SM`, `PO`, `Team member`) are organizational/provenance metadata only; do not add login, user accounts, sessions, or permission checks.
3. **No Capacity Calculations, Deduction Mode, or Capacity Planner (`F004`)**: Do not compute `NETWORKDAYS`, member dev days, team dev days, normalized velocity, forecast SP, category budgets, or load indicators. Do not implement `Team.deduction_mode` (`percent` | `fixed_days`) or expose Team/per-location `hours_per_day` editing controls.
4. **No Full Team Calendar UI or Operational Holiday/Absence Editing (`F005`)**: Do not build the Team Calendar grid, day-to-day absence entry/editing, holiday CRUD/refresh/cancellation workflow UI (e.g., cancelling a library holiday as a company working day), SprintCeremony, Meeting, Milestone, or planned-overtime editing UI.
5. **No Work Categories or Allocations (`F003`)**: Do not create Category or TeamCategoryAllocation tables, defaults, overrides, or 100% allocation validation.
6. **No PI/Sprint Commitments, Plan Versioning, or Lifecycle Transitions (`F012`, `F013`, `F015`)**: Do not implement `Start PI`, `Start Sprint`, `Close Sprint`, `PlanVersion`, `PICommitment`, `SprintCommitment`, `SprintClosure`/`Actuals`, or transitions of `PI.lifecycle_status`.
7. **No Team Planning Style or Checklist Templates (`F007`, `F013`)**: Do not configure `planning_style` (`owner` | `distributed`) or DoR/DoD `ChecklistTemplate` records.
8. **No Work Items, Projects, Features, or Boards (`F006`–`F019`)**: Do not implement Projects, Features, Stories, Bugs, Tasks, Dependencies, or operational planning boards.
9. **No Assignment History Editing UI**: `member_team_assignments` is maintained automatically on Member create/move; editing/backdating history and a history UI are deferred.
10. **No Per-Location Workday Hours, Self-Service Leave, or `Business Owner` Role**: Deferred per `AZUSA_HANDOFF_UPDATE_2026-11-20.md` and Clarification Q4.

## User Behavior

### User Story 1 — ART, Team & Member Management with Team Moves (Priority: P1)

As the local Azusa operator, I want to create, edit, organize, archive/reactivate, move Members between active Teams with dated history, and safely delete (when unreferenced) ARTs, Teams (with permitted ±1 working-day Sprint offsets), and Members (with roles, FTE, Location, and avatar colour) on `/organization` so my organizational hierarchy is accurately persisted and ready for downstream features.

**Why this priority**: ARTs, Teams, and Members are the root organizational entities required by every subsequent feature (`F003`–`F019`) (AC01, AC02, AC03, AC04, AC13).

**Independent Test**: Open `/organization`, create two ARTs (e.g., `Payments ART` and `Core Platform ART`), edit one ART's name, reload and confirm persistence; create two Teams under `Payments ART` (one with `sprint_offset_workdays = -1`, one with `0`) and one Team under `Core Platform ART`, edit a Team's name/offset and reload to confirm persistence; verify invalid offsets (`-2`, `+2`) are rejected and default `hours_per_day = 8.0` is preserved; add 15 Members (exceeding the legacy 13-member spreadsheet cap) spanning multiple Locations (`PH`, `SG`, `IN`, `UK`) with roles including `SM`, `PO`, and `Team member` and varied `fte_factor` values (`1.0`, `0.5`), edit a Member and reload to confirm persistence; move a Member to a Team in `Core Platform ART` with an `effectiveDate` after its initial `start_date` and confirm role/FTE/Location stay with the Member while the old Team cannot be hard-deleted because of the historical assignment; archive a Member and a Team (verifying children are not auto-archived and archived records remain inspectable), reactivate them (confirming child reactivation under an archived parent is rejected), verify hard-deleting a Team with Members or historical assignments is blocked, and verify hard-deleting an empty mistake ART succeeds.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the operator is on `/organization`, **When** they create an ART with a non-empty name (case-insensitively unique), **Then** the ART is persisted with a UUIDv4 `id`, `record_status = 'active'`, and F001 audit fields (`created_at`, `updated_at`, `created_by = 'local:sm'`, `updated_by = 'local:sm'`), and appears in the ART list.

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST provide CRUD + archive/reactivate (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`) for **ARTs** (`trains`), where each ART has a case-insensitively unique trimmed non-empty name, UUIDv4 primary key, and F001 audit columns (`created_at`, `updated_at`, `created_by`, `updated_by`).
- **FR-002**: System MUST provide CRUD + archive/reactivate (`record_status`) for **Teams** (`teams`), enforcing that every Team belongs to **exactly one ART** (`train_id` immutable after creation), has a case-insensitively unique trimmed name within its ART, stores `sprint_offset_workdays` strictly as an integer in `{-1, 0, +1}` (default `0`), and preserves `hours_per_day` defaulting to `8.0`.
- **FR-003**: System MUST provide CRUD, date-resolved Team moves (current, future-dated, same-day correction, and cancel pending move), and archive/reactivate (`record_status`) for **Members** (`members`), with `member_team_assignments` as the sole source of truth for Team membership (`members.team_id` removed) and each Member referencing **one Location** (active on create/change) with no roster cap. Member create MUST open a `member_team_assignments` row starting on server local `today` (`end_date = null`). Moving a Member (`teamId`, optional `effectiveDate` defaulting to `today`) MUST validate destination Team is active (`409` otherwise): reject `effectiveDate < today` (`400`, no backdating, `OD-11`); reject `effectiveDate < latestRow.start_date` when a pending row exists (`400` stating pending move must be cancelled first); if `effectiveDate === latestRow.start_date` (`OD-9`, only valid for rows starting `today` or pending rows) delete the latest row and reopen the preceding assignment when correcting back to the preceding Team, or update `latestRow.team_id` in place for any other correction. The preceding Team must be active when correcting today's move; if `effectiveDate > latestRow.start_date` reject (`409`) if a pending row already exists, else close `latestRow` at `effectiveDate - 1 day` and insert the new open row (`OD-8`). Cancelling a pending move MUST delete the future row and reopen the immediately preceding row (`end_date = null`).
- **FR-004**: Domain layer (`src/domain/organization/roles.ts`) MUST expose an authoritative, pure capacity-eligibility rule (`contributesToDevCapacity(role)`) returning `false` for `PO` and `SM` and `true` for `Admin`, `RTE`, and `Team member`, enforced via domain enum and SQL `CHECK` constraint, without implementing capacity calculations or RBAC/authentication.
- **FR-005**: System MUST provide CRUD + archive/reactivate (`record_status: 'active' | 'archived'`) for **PIs** (`pis`), enforcing single-ART ownership, explicit case-insensitively unique `name` within ART, explicit ISO calendar dates `start_date <= end_date`, a separate `lifecycle_status` (`planning` | `in_progress` | `closed`, default `planning`, not transitioned in F002), and **no overlapping dates among active (`record_status = 'active'`) PIs within the same ART** (gaps permitted; cross-ART overlaps permitted).
- **FR-006**: System MUST provide CRUD for **Iterations** (`iterations`) belonging to a single PI, storing explicit case-insensitively unique `name` within PI, `kind` ∈ `{'development', 'ip'}`, and explicit **Monday–Friday weekday** ISO dates `start_date <= end_date` (Saturday or Sunday rejected with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`). System MUST enforce **no overlapping Iterations within the same PI** and **full containment within parent PI dates** (`pi.start_date <= iteration.start_date` and `iteration.end_date <= pi.end_date`), while permitting gaps, arbitrary durations, arbitrary development Sprint counts, and optional `ip` iterations.
- **FR-007**: Domain layer MUST provide an optional pure helper computing the `YYWW` label from an Iteration's `end_date` using ISO-8601 week numbering (`computeIsoCalendarWeek`), and MUST NEVER derive or mutate dates from a PI or Iteration name.
- **FR-008**: Domain layer MUST compute **Team-specific scheduled Sprint boundaries** from a canonical ART Iteration's weekday `[start_date, end_date]` and the Team's `sprint_offset_workdays` (`-1`, `0`, `+1`) by shifting each date by Monday–Friday weekdays only (skipping Saturday and Sunday), returning a distinct `TeamIterationSchedule` view linked to the unchanged canonical `iteration_id` and canonical ART dates.

## Edge Cases

- **Weekday-only Sprint boundaries & Team ±1 working-day offset (`OD-1`, `OD-2`)**:
  - Canonical Iteration `start_date` or `end_date` falling on Saturday or Sunday is rejected with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
  - Canonical start on Monday with `-1` shifts to the previous **Friday** (1 working day before the canonical PI start — valid and matched by `TodayWidget` per `OD-2`).
  - Canonical end on Friday with `+1` shifts to the next **Monday** (1 working day after the canonical PI end — valid and matched by `TodayWidget` per `OD-2`).
  - Because Iterations in a PI (and active PIs in an ART) cannot overlap or touch and every Sprint boundary is Mon–Fri, a Team's shifted Sprints never share a day; on crafted invalid data where two shifted Sprints overlap on today, `resolveTodayCadenceStatus` breaks ties by preferring the Sprint whose canonical range contains today.
- **PI & Iteration date boundaries**:
  - Adjacent PIs (`PI1.end_date = 2027-03-12`, `PI2.start_date = 2027-03-15`) and gaps are allowed; touching on the same date (`PI1.end_date === PI2.start_date`) is an overlap and is rejected among active PIs in the same ART.
  - Editing a PI's dates so an existing child Iteration falls outside `[pi.start_date, pi.end_date]` is rejected (`409`).
  - Different ARTs may have identical or overlapping PI and Iteration dates without conflict.
- **ISO-8601 `YYWW` year boundaries**:
  - A Sprint ending on `2026-12-31` or `2027-01-01` uses ISO-8601 week-year (`computeIsoCalendarWeek`); user edits to the Sprint name never alter dates.
  - Today widget Sprint prefix rule (`OD-5`): `"2706"` → `"Sprint 2706"`, `"Sprint 5"` → `"Sprint 5"`, `"sprint 12"` → `"sprint 12"` (no duplicate `"Sprint"`).
- **Archiving, reactivation, moves & deletion (`OD-3`, `OD-8`, `OD-9`, `OD-11`)**:
  - Archiving an ART does **not** auto-archive its Teams or PIs; archiving a Team does **not** auto-archive its Members (and is blocked with `409` if the Team has pending incoming moves); archiving a Location does **not** strip existing Members or delete Holidays.
  - New Teams/PIs under an archived ART, new Members under an archived Team or archived Location, new Iterations under an archived PI, and reactivating a child whose parent ART/current Team is archived are all rejected (`409`); a Member whose Location is archived **can** be reactivated.
  - Moving a Member with `effectiveDate < today` is rejected (`400`, no backdating); moving to an archived Team is rejected (`409`); moving an archived Member to an active Team is allowed; cancelling a pending move — whether via `DELETE /pending-move` or by `PATCH`ing the pending move's destination back to the immediately preceding Team — is checked **before** the active-destination-Team check and succeeds even if the original Team has since been archived.
  - Hard deletion of an ART (with Teams or PIs), Team (with any past, current, or pending `member_team_assignments`), PI (with Iterations), Member (with Absences), or Location (with Members or Holidays) is blocked (`409`); deleting an unreferenced Member removes its own assignment rows cleanly.
- **Holiday provenance, overrides & jurisdiction edits (`F3`, `F5`)**:
  - Re-seeding library holidays uses partial unique identity `(location_id, date, name) WHERE source = 'library'`, never duplicating library rows or overwriting `company`/`manual` overrides.
  - When a `company` or `manual` row exists on `(location_id, date)`, it supersedes `library` rows on that date in effective holiday queries.
  - Editing a Location's `country_code` or `subdivision_code` replaces only future (`date >= today`) `library` rows, preserving historical library rows and all `company`/`manual` overrides.
  - Islamic/Hijri moon-sighting holidays are stored with `confirmed = false`; fixed and computable holidays (including SG Chinese New Year) are stored with `confirmed = true`.
- **Absences validation (`OD-10`)**:
  - Null, empty, or malformed `date` is rejected (`400`); active conflict checks apply **only** among capacity-reducing (`affects_capacity = true`) Absences on the same `(member_id, date)` (e.g., `AL full + HD am`, `AL full + SL full`, or duplicate active `HD am` are rejected with `409`), while attendance-only types (`WFH`, `ON_SITE`, `BT`) never conflict (e.g., `WFH full + HD pm` and `ON_SITE full + SL pm` are allowed).
- **Empty / partial organizational context & `/organization` header (`OD-6`, `OD-7`)**:
  - Zero active Teams across all active ARTs: header omits `ART › Team › PI` breadcrumb, profile subtitle shows `"No ART or team configured"`, Today widget shows `"No active PI · No active Sprint"`.
  - An ART with zero active Teams does not appear in the header (its PIs are managed on `/organization`).
  - On `/organization`: header shows only logo, search (`"Search organization…"`), and profile (no Today widget, no context breadcrumb).
  - Selected Team/PI is archived or deleted: header context gracefully reconciles to an available active Team/PI or unconfigured state without crashing.

## Data Considerations

### 1. Persistence Conventions & Constitution Principle III

- All new tables live in local PGlite storage managed by Drizzle ORM migrations (`src/persistence/migrations/0001_organization_cadence.sql`), using F001's `auditColumns()` convention: UUIDv4 primary keys (`text`, `crypto.randomUUID()`), UTC `created_at` / `updated_at`, and `created_by` / `updated_by` defaulting to `"local:sm"` (internal-only override, never accepted from HTTP bodies).
- Calendar dates (`PI.start_date`, `PI.end_date`, `Iteration.start_date`, `Iteration.end_date`, `Holiday.date`, `Absence.date`, `MemberTeamAssignment.start_date`, `MemberTeamAssignment.end_date`) are stored as timezone-independent ISO-8601 calendar date strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- All archive flags use a consistent `record_status` (`'active' | 'archived'`) column across `trains`, `teams`, `members`, `pis`, and `locations`, keeping `pis.lifecycle_status` (`'planning' | 'in_progress' | 'closed'`) separate for F012.
- All name/code uniqueness constraints (`trains.name`, `(teams.train_id, teams.name)`, `(pis.train_id, pis.name)`, `(iterations.pi_id, iterations.name)`, `locations.code`, `absence_types.code`) are **case-insensitive** (`lower(trim(...))`).
- **Constitution Principle III compliance**: F002 introduces **only** organizational, team-assignment history, and calendar configuration/reference tables. It creates **zero** `PlanVersion`, `PICommitment`, `SprintCommitment`, `SprintClosure`/`Actuals`, or `StateChange` records. Canonical ART Iteration dates are stored once on `Iteration`, while Team scheduled Sprint boundaries are derived from `Team.sprint_offset_workdays`. `ON DELETE RESTRICT` + soft-archive (`record_status`) + `member_team_assignments` protect historical integrity.
